SPG 1.2 — SPEED PROGRESSION V02
================================

STATUS: READY FOR PLAYTEST

CALIBRATION V02
---------------
Natural acceleration:
- 0 km/h at start
- ~7 km/h at 5s
- ~11 km/h at 10s
- ~15 km/h at 20s
- ~19 km/h at 35s
- ~22 km/h at 60s
- ~24 km/h at 90s

Pump:
- Lower base gain than V01
- 100% gain through 18 km/h
- ~70% around 22 km/h
- ~40% around 25 km/h
- ~22% around 27 km/h
- ~10% around 29 km/h
- almost residual from 29→30 km/h

Progressive maximum speed:
- 0s: ~14 km/h ceiling
- 15s: ~20 km/h
- 30s: ~23 km/h
- 45s: ~25 km/h
- 60s: ~27 km/h
- 90s: 30 km/h

Result intended:
- The game reaches a satisfying cruising rhythm much earlier.
- 30 km/h can no longer be reached prematurely through aggressive pumping.
- Pump remains clearly useful, but top speed becomes an earned late-run state.
- Existing speed-perception effects from V01 remain intact.

LOCKED / UNCHANGED:
- jump physics
- hitboxes
- obstacle behavior
- ranking / Supabase
- GA4
- UI and final screens
